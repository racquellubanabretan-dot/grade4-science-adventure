📚 About Science Adventure

This interactive game is a supplementary review and practice resource based on the Grade 4 Science Second Term scope used for this project. It is designed to reinforce classroom learning through guided practice and game-based review.

🎮 How to Play

Choose a mission and answer 15 randomly selected questions. Correct answers earn points, streaks, and stars. Wrong answers use a life and show the correct answer with a rationale.

🎲 Built for Repeat Practice

Question order and answer positions are randomized for each game, so learners can replay missions for additional practice.
