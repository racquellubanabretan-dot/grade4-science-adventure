# Grade 4 Science Adventure v0.3 — Polished Review Build

A curriculum-aligned interactive Grade 4 Science Second Term review game by RAAL Educational Resources.

## Coverage
- Mission 1: Life Cycles of Animals
- Mission 2: Animals, Food & Food Chains
- Mission 3: Forces and Movement
- Mission 4: Magnets and Non-Contact Forces

## Game features
- 15 randomized questions per mission
- Randomized answer positions
- 3 lives, score, streaks, and stars
- Explanations/rationales
- Science Explorer Profile and badges
- Mobile-friendly, offline play
- Curriculum information and replay guidance

This is a supplementary review and practice resource and does not replace official DepEd learning resources or classroom instruction.
